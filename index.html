A council claims five years of housing supply. Your client thinks the number is wrong. We break down the claim by site type and delivery rate and show you where the gap is. You get a one-page analysis with the real figure and the evidence behind it.

How this works under the hood

We use a Bayesian spatial model (BYM2) that covers all 6,800+ middle-layer super output areas in England. It combines EPC, Census 2021, IMD and fuel poverty data to produce rankings that account for local variation rather than averaging over large areas.

The methodology is currently under peer review. We also build live dashboards and evaluation frameworks for place-based programmes.
